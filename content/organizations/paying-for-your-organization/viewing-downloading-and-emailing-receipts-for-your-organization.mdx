As an organization owner, you can view, download, and email receipts for the complete billing history of your organization.

## Viewing receipts

1. <Shared id="user-login" />

   <Shared id="user-login" image />

2. <Shared id="account-settings" />

   <Shared id="account-settings" image />

3. In the left sidebar, click the name of the organization whose billing receipts you want to view.

   <Shared id="organization-selection" image />

4. <Shared id="organization-billing-tab" />

   <Shared id="organization-billing-tab" image />

5. <Shared id="billing-history" />

   <Shared id="billing-history" image />

6. To view a single receipt, find the row of the receipt you want to view, then, on the right side of the row, click the view icon.

7. <Shared id="billing-view" />

   <Shared id="billing-view" image />
